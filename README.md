## Additional Research

### Creative Reasoning & Long-Context Model Behavior

An exploratory study involving an extended creative dialogue between a human author and an AI model.

The experiment began with an unusual causal reasoning challenge connecting a streetlight pillar and kefir, and developed into a fictional scientific framework.

The dialogue provides material for examining creative collaboration, context retention, adaptation to feedback, and potential sycophantic behavior.

The experiment was incorporated into the illustrated novel *Kefir and the Pillar*.

## Methodology

My independent evaluations generally follow a structured process:

1. Define the reasoning capability or behavioral pattern to investigate.
2. Construct an original test scenario.
3. Observe the model's initial response.
4. Introduce follow-up questions or challenges.
5. Analyze assumptions, inconsistencies, and behavioral changes.
6. Document observations and limitations.
